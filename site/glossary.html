Glossary

Plain-English definitions, in the order a beginner actually needs them.

Assistant — the brain. The software that hears you, figures out what you want, and does it. Also called "voice assistant."

Wake word — the magic word ("Alexa," "Hey Google," "Hey Siri") that turns on listening. Everything before the wake word is ignored; everything after is processed.

Voice profile / Voice Match / Voice ID — what lets the speaker tell you apart from your partner. Per person, set up in the app.

Skill (Alexa) / Action (Google) — a third-party mini-app for your speaker: recipes, trivia, the ability to order Domino's. Enabled in the app.

Routine (Alexa/Google) / Shortcut (Apple) — a recipe: one phrase or schedule triggers several actions.

Reminder — a message the assistant says to you at a time you specify. One-shot or repeating.

Timer — a countdown. Multiple named timers can run at once.

Alarm — a wake-up call. Repeats daily by default.

Do Not Disturb (DND) — mode where the speaker won't ping you with reminders and notifications, but still responds to commands and alarm timers.

Drop-In (Alexa) — a feature that lets household devices call your speaker and listen live-ish. Useful for check-ins with elderly family, creepy if you didn't know you'd turned it on.

Household (Google/Amazon) — the list of accounts that can use your speaker. Prune occasionally.

Guest Connect (Alexa) — lets visiting guests use their own Alexa on your speaker.

Personal Requests (HomePod) — lets the HomePod read your calendar, texts, etc., through your iCloud account. Per-person setting.

Voice Purchasing (Alexa) / Voice purchases (Google) — the setting that lets the speaker actually spend your money.

Auto-delete — a setting that wipes old voice recordings after a set number of months.

Mic mute button — the physical button that makes the speaker stop listening to the room. Emits red light or a tone when active.

DND / Do Not Disturb mode — see above.

Prime / reorder list (Alexa) — Amazon's list of things you buy repeatedly; Amazon can restrict voice shopping to just this list.

Family Link (Google) — Google's parental-control app; ties a kid's Google account to yours.

Kids+ (Amazon) — Amazon's paid kids' content/skill bundle, plus the parental dashboard.

2.4GHz / 5GHz Wi-Fi — the two networks your router broadcasts. Speakers like 2.4GHz more because it goes through walls better.

Deregister (Alexa) — unlinking the speaker from your account; the "start over" moment.

A two-way conversation vs. one-way audio — some things (like calling) cause "two-way" audio. Some things (like streaming music) are one-way. Some privacy features only apply to one-way.

A voice you didn't set up — in our guide's terms, anyone whose voice the speaker wasn't told to recognize. See shopping scams.
