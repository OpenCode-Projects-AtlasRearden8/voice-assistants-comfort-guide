# Welcome: Smart Speakers, Explained Like a Friend Would

If you've ever shouted "Hey Alexa!" only to have the speaker do something *closely* related to what you asked, this guide is for you. No jargon, no condescension — just the stuff worth knowing, in the order it's actually useful.

## Who this is for

You (or someone you love) has a smart speaker — an Amazon Echo with Alexa, a Google Nest speaker, or an Apple HomePod with Siri — or is thinking about one. Maybe it was a gift. Maybe it's still in the box. Maybe it's currently sitting there judging you silently. All of these are valid starting points.

## How to use this guide

- Start with [Setup in One Weekend](01-setup.md) if the speaker is still new to you.
- Skim [Privacy Settings Worth Touching](02-privacy.md) before you get comfortable — it takes 15 minutes and saves headaches.
- Learn [Daily Commands That Actually Earn Their Keep](03-daily-commands.md) and keep the [Command Cheat Sheets](cheat-sheets.md) nearby.
- Set up [Routines: Reminders and Timers on Autopilot](04-routines.md) once you have the basics down.
- Read [Voice Shopping Without Getting Burned](05-shopping.md) so the first thing your speaker buys isn't a pallet of who-knows-what.
- Handle [Kids and Guests](06-kids-guests.md) before they handle you.
- When things go wrong: [My Speaker Stopped Listening](07-troubleshooting.md).
- And before you buy a second (or third) speaker, read [When Your Phone Just Does It Better](08-phone-vs-speaker.md).

## The one-sentence version of the whole guide

A smart speaker is just a voice remote for helpful things — set it up, lock down the few settings that matter, teach it a handful of words, and let it earn its spot on the counter.
