# Command Cheat Sheets

Print these pages or save them to your phone. Four cheat sheets: one per brand, plus a universal translation table.

## Alexa (Echo)

| To do this | Say this |
|---|---|
| Send reminder | "Alexa, remind me to ___ at ___."
| Repeating reminder | "Alexa, remind me to ___ every day at ___."
| Timer | "Alexa, set a 10 minute timer."
| Named timer | "Alexa, set a pasta timer for 10 minutes."
| Check timers | "Alexa, how much time is left?"
| Cancel timer | "Alexa, cancel the pasta timer."
| Alarm | "Alexa, set an alarm for 6:30."
| Wake alarm snooze | "Snooze."
| Music | "Alexa, play some jazz."
| Volume | "Alexa, volume 4." (1–10) |
| Weather | "Alexa, what's the weather?"
| Weather elsewhere | "Alexa, what's the weather in [city]?"
| Math / conversion | "Alexa, what's 15% of 60?" / "Convert 3 cups to tablespoons."
| Recipe help | "Alexa, how many ounces in a cup?"
| Call contact | "Alexa, call Mom."
| Find my phone | "Alexa, find my phone."
| Stop talking | "Alexa, stop."
| Mute mic | press the button with a mic icon / circle-with-line
| Morning routine setup | app → More → Routines → + → "Good morning."
| Shopping on / off | app → Settings → Voice Purchasing
| Delete voice history | app → Alexa Privacy → Review Voice History → Delete
| Do Not Disturb | "Alexa, do not disturb until 7am."

## Google (Nest speakers)

| To do this | Say this |
|---|---|
| Send reminder | "Hey Google, remind me to ___ at ___."
| Repeating reminder | "Hey Google, remind me to ___ every Sunday at 4."
| Timer | "Hey Google, set a 10 minute timer."
| Named timer | "Hey Google, set a pasta timer for 10 minutes."
| Check timers | "Hey Google, how much time is left?"
| Cancel timer | "Hey Google, cancel the pasta timer."
| Alarm | "Hey Google, set an alarm for 6:30."
| Wake alarm snooze | "Snooze."
| Music | "Hey Google, play lo-fi beats."
| Volume | "Hey Google, volume 4." (1–10) |
| Weather | "Hey Google, what's the weather?"
| Math | "Hey Google, what's 15% of 60?"
| Recipe help | "Hey Google, how many ounces in a cup?"
| Call contact | "Hey Google, call Mom."
| Find my phone | "Hey Google, find my phone."
| Stop talking | "Hey Google, stop."
| Mute mic | press the mic mute switch on the top/underside
| Morning routine setup | app → Automations → + → "Good morning."
| Shopping on / off | Google account → Payments → "On voice purchases" Off/PIN
| Delete voice history | myactivity.google.com → filter "Assistant" → Delete
| Do Not Disturb | "Hey Google, do not disturb."

## Translation table (same idea, different words)

| You want | Alexa | Google | Siri |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wake word | Alexa / Echo / Computer | Hey Google / OK Google | Hey Siri |
| Volume scale | 0–10 | 0–10 | 0–100% |
| Check a timer | "how much time is left" | "how much time is left" | "how much time is left" |
| Repeat a reminder | "every day/firm date" | "every day/weekday" | "daily / every [day]" |
| App for settings | Amazon Alexa | Google Home | Home (iPhone) |
| Delete recordings by default | Review Voice History | myactivity.google.com | Delete Siri & Dictation History |
